## Not recovered
The remaining piece(s) (before `2_5t3@1_` and after `u_n33d_`). Things tried and ruled out:
* LSB / 2-LSB planes (L, R, interleaved, both bit orders): no ASCII; chi-square of value pairs flat. Right channel before 22.7 s is
  pure +-14 LSB noise-shaped dither (no tones, no narrow-band peaks).
* Spectrograms of L/R/L-R at several window sizes, per-bin-normalised, incl. >15 kHz and the quiet intro/outro: no further text.
* 0-22.6 s of L is a spoken/sung vocal ("turn it up", f0~160 Hz, 11 phrases); faster-whisper only returns lyric text / hallucinations.
* 25-34.4 s (L==R + independent dither) is a noisy, vowel/fricative-like high-passed layer (energy 4.5-18 kHz, harmonic spacing ~75 Hz).
  Tried spectral inversion and ring-mod/SSB demodulation for carriers 2-21 kHz (fine scan 7.5-8.1 kHz) and slow-down 1.5-4x, then ASR:
  no intelligible message ("sweet / good / you / choo choo choo" hallucinations).
* ID3 extended-header CRC and WAV chunk slack: nothing.
Not tried: steghide-style keyed embedding (steghide is not installable in this sandbox; `12345678` would be the obvious passphrase to try
on a machine that has it: `steghide extract -sf "12345678 (1).wav" -p 12345678`), or a human listening to the vocal/tail.
